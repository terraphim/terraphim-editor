# lab-filler

Words and phrases that add emphasis or padding but no meaning. Each entry must be safe to fade on its own: removing it (with its leading space, and a bracketing comma where present) leaves a grammatical sentence. Used by "Mark hedges and filler" and as word-level trim candidates.

The concept name is deliberately prefixed `lab-` so that, when this file is compiled by the terraphim_automata thesaurus builder (which also adds the concept name itself as a pattern), the name never matches ordinary prose.

synonyms:: really, very, quite, just, actually, basically, simply, truly, literally, certainly, definitely, totally, of course, i mean, at all, a bit, a little bit, pretty much, sort of, kind of, in fact, indeed, needless to say, fairly, all of a sudden, as a matter of fact, as it were
