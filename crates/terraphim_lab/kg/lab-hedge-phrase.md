# lab-hedge-phrase

Hedges that are part of the verb phrase. Removing them alone would break the sentence or change its meaning, so they are mark-only: they count towards hedge density (and so towards sentence weakness) but are never trim candidates on their own.

synonyms:: seems to, seem to, seemed to, it seems, it seemed, appears to, appear to, appeared to, might, could be, may be, tend to, tends to, likely, unlikely, almost, nearly, roughly, approximately, i am not sure, not quite
