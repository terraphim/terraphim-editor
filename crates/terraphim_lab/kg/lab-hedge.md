# lab-hedge

Hedging adverbs and parenthetical hedges that can be faded without breaking the sentence. Used by "Mark hedges and filler" and as word-level trim candidates.

synonyms:: perhaps, maybe, probably, possibly, arguably, apparently, presumably, seemingly, somewhat, somehow, generally, evidently, i fancy, i suppose, i think, i believe, i guess, it would appear, so to speak, more or less, to some extent, in a sense, in some ways
