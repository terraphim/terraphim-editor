# lab-tone

Sample "off-tone" list for a neutral, professional register: informal and slang words. Used only by "Mark words that don't fit the tone" (mark-only, never a trim candidate). A real deployment would ship one list per register (professional, academic, casual) and let the active role choose.

synonyms:: stuff, gonna, wanna, kinda, sorta, awesome, super, chum, guys, cool, okay, lots of, a lot of, tons of, folks, nope, yeah, totally awesome, no-brainer, deal-breaker
